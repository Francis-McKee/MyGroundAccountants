# MyGroundAccountants

A business landing page developed to establish an online presence and provide a direct inquiry channel for a specialized accounting service.

**Live Website:** [MyGroundAccountants](https://mygroundaccountants.com/)

## Project Overview

MyGroundAccountants needed a dedicated online presence to communicate its specialized accounting services to prospective clients.

I developed a business landing page using HTML and CSS, incorporating the client's existing logo and color scheme to maintain brand consistency.

Beyond website development, I implemented a Formspree contact form to allow prospective clients to submit inquiries, integrated Google Analytics, and established the business's Google Business Profile.

## The Business Problem

Without a dedicated website, MyGroundAccountants lacked a centralized online destination where prospective clients could learn about its accounting services and contact the business.

The project presented an opportunity to establish a professional digital presence that clearly communicated the business's services while providing a direct way for potential customers to initiate contact.

## My Solution

I developed a focused business landing page designed to introduce the accounting service and encourage prospective clients to submit inquiries.

My responsibilities included:

### Website Development

- **Landing page development:** Built a business landing page using HTML and CSS.
- **Brand consistency:** Incorporated the client's existing logo and color scheme into the website design.
- **Lead generation:** Implemented a Formspree-powered contact form to allow visitors to submit inquiries directly through the website.
- **Website analytics:** Integrated Google Analytics to measure website activity.

### Google Business Profile

I created the business's Google Business Profile to establish its presence in Google Search and Maps.

My responsibilities included setting up the profile and entering the business information, with the company biography left for the client to provide.

### Third-Party Service Integration

Implementing the contact form required coordinating with Formspree, an external form-processing service.

One challenge was getting the client registered with Formspree so the contact form could be connected and configured for use.

This required coordinating the technical implementation with an additional client-side setup requirement.

## Results and Impact

The project delivered a dedicated business landing page and established additional infrastructure for the business's online presence.

Key deliverables included:

- A custom business landing page.
- Website styling consistent with the client's existing brand identity.
- A functional inquiry mechanism using Formspree.
- Google Analytics integration for website activity measurement.
- A newly created Google Business Profile.

No verified traffic, lead-generation, or conversion figures are currently available for this project.

## Project Challenges and Lessons Learned

### Third-Party Form Integration

Implementing the contact form involved more than writing the frontend code. The client also needed to register with Formspree before the integration could be fully configured.

This highlighted an important aspect of client-facing development: successful delivery sometimes depends on coordinating external services and guiding clients through the setup process.
